Welcome — how to read these wireframes

These are simple screen sketches of the LMC system. They show what each person will see and do, not the final colours or design. Use the menu on the left, or the Next / Previous buttons at the bottom of every page, to walk through the screens.

1 Orange numbersEach screen has orange numbered markers. The panel on the right explains what each numbered part does.
Grey striped boxesThese are placeholders for photos, maps or charts. The real content will appear there.
Sample dataNames, numbers and addresses are dummy examples to make the screens easier to read.
Fields still to be finalisedExact form fields for the five field steps will be shared by Gujarat Energy Ltd. Where you see "example field", the final list will replace it.
Final look & feelColours, fonts and branding are decided in the UI/UX design stage, after these wireframes are agreed.

How a PNG connection moves through the system

The journey of one house, from the Service Order in SAP to the final commissioning.

SAP

Service Order created

Created in Gujarat Energy Ltd's existing SAP — not in this system.

Automatic sync

Arrives in LMC

Service Order, contractor, customer and house details come in from SAP.

Contractor

Shares leads

Creates Supervisors and assigns the household leads to them.

Supervisor

Assigns a team

Gives each house to a Plumber Team (or does small jobs personally).

Plumber Team

Does 5 steps

Fills each step's form with photos, GPS and meter data, then submits.

Supervisor

Approves

Approves each step, or sends it back for rework with a remark.

When the house is commissioned — or cancelled

CommissionedCancelled→ status is updated for the Contractor and the Admin, and sent back to the SAP portal and the PNGRB portal.

Watching everything, at all times

Gujarat Energy Ltd Admin all GAs   GA Admin own GA only   — live status, activity logs, disputes, cancellations and reports.

If there is a problem at site

Plumber Team raises a Dispute with a reason → Supervisor either marks it Resolved (work continues) or Cancels the connection → Contractor & Admin are notified.

Status of a house (lead)

Every house carries one clear status. The same status is shown to everyone — Admin, GA Admin, Contractor, Supervisor and Plumber Team. Final names can be adjusted during discovery.

Not assigned→With Supervisor→With Plumber Team→Step 1…5 in progress→Awaiting approval→Commissioned
Not assigned
House has arrived from SAP; Contractor has not yet given it to a Supervisor.
With Supervisor
Supervisor has it, but no Plumber Team is assigned yet.
Step 2 of 5
Plumber Team is working on this step.
Awaiting approval
A step has been submitted and is waiting for the Supervisor.
Rework
Supervisor sent the step back with a remark. Plumber Team must correct and resubmit.
Disputed
Plumber Team reported a problem at site. Waiting for the Supervisor's decision.
Commissioned
All five steps approved. Status is sent back to SAP and PNGRB.
Cancelled
Supervisor cancelled the work for this customer (with a reason).
House locked
House Lock status as received from SAP, shown inside the platform.
